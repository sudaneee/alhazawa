The Challenge

Millions of children are waiting for a chance.

Across underserved communities in Northern Nigeria, orphaned children and vulnerable girls face overlapping barriers to education, welfare and opportunity — barriers that compound each other, quietly, for years.

  • Loss of parental careOrphaned children often lose not just guardianship, but stability, advocacy and continuity of schooling.
  • Poverty & economic hardshipBasic needs compete directly with school fees, materials and time — and basic needs usually win.
  • Barriers specific to the girl-childCultural expectations, early marriage risk and domestic responsibility disproportionately push girls out of the classroom.
  • Insecurity & lack of infrastructureIn communities with too few schools and too little protection, exclusion becomes the default, not the exception.
